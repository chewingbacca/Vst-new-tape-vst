Verdant for Windows (64-bit)
============================

1. Copy the whole folder "Verdant.vst3" to
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
   (create the VST3 folder if it does not exist).
2. Start your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Verdant appears as an effect by "Verdant Audio Lab".

"Verdant.exe" is the standalone version: it runs without a DAW and processes your
audio input. Use headphones to avoid feedback.

This build is not code-signed. If Windows SmartScreen warns about Verdant.exe,
choose "More info" > "Run anyway".
