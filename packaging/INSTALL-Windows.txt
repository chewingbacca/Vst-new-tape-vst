Automata 1.0 for Windows (64-bit)
=================================

VST3 plug-in
  Copy the folder "Automata.vst3" to
      C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  (you need administrator rights for that folder), then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.
  Automata appears as an instrument (Instrument | Drum | Sampler | Synth).

Standalone
  Run Automata.exe directly. Choose your audio and MIDI devices with the
  "Options" button at the top-left of the window.

Windows SmartScreen
  The files are not code-signed, so Windows may warn that the app is from an
  unknown publisher. Choose "More info" -> "Run anyway" for the standalone.
  The VST3 loads in your DAW without that prompt.

Getting started
  Press Play in the DAW (or Space in the plug-in), then NEW IDEA. Browse the 40
  factory kits with the < and > buttons. The full guide is in README.md in the
  source repository.
