Automata 1.0 for macOS (Universal: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later)
===============================================================================

Install
  VST3:        copy Automata.vst3      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  Audio Unit:  copy Automata.component to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  Standalone:  copy Automata.app       to  /Applications (or anywhere)

  (~/Library is hidden: in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the path.)

First launch: "Automata can't be opened / is damaged"
  These builds are not notarised by Apple, so macOS quarantines files that came
  from the internet. Remove the quarantine flag once, in Terminal:

      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Automata.vst3
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Automata.component
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Automata.app

  Then restart your DAW (Logic: it rescans Audio Units on launch; if Automata
  does not appear, open Logic > Settings > Plug-in Manager and rescan).

Getting started
  Press Play in the DAW (or Space in the plug-in), then NEW IDEA. Browse the 40
  factory kits with the < and > buttons. The full guide is in README.md in the
  source repository.
